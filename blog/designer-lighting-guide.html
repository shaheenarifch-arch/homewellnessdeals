Home Design Guide

How to Choose Designer Lighting for Your Home

The right fixture provides practical illumination while also becoming part of a room's overall design. Use this guide to choose lighting by room, size fixtures correctly and create a balanced lighting plan.

The Three Main Types of Home Lighting

A well-designed room often combines several types of lighting rather than relying on one fixture.

Ambient Lighting

Ambient lighting provides general illumination throughout a room. Ceiling fixtures, chandeliers and other broad light sources can contribute to ambient lighting.

Task Lighting

Task lighting is designed around specific activities. Examples include lighting for kitchen preparation areas, reading spaces, desks and bathroom mirrors.

Accent and Decorative Lighting

Accent lighting can highlight architectural details, artwork or decorative features. Decorative fixtures can also function as visual focal points.

How to Choose Lighting by Room

Living Room Lighting

Living rooms often benefit from layered lighting. Consider combining general illumination with lamps, accent lighting or a statement fixture. The size of the room and furniture should influence the scale of the main fixture.

Dining Room Lighting

Pendant lights and chandeliers are common choices for dining spaces. Consider the size of the dining table and the fixture's proportions. The light should complement the table rather than overwhelm it.

Kitchen Lighting

Kitchen lighting needs to balance atmosphere and functionality. General lighting can be combined with task lighting around preparation and cooking areas.

Bedroom Lighting

Bedroom lighting generally benefits from a softer, comfortable atmosphere. Consider a combination of ambient lighting and bedside or reading lighting.

Entryways and Hallways

Entryways can provide an opportunity to introduce the design style of the rest of the home. Consider ceiling height, available space and the scale of the fixture before purchasing.

How to Choose the Right Size Light Fixture

Scale is one of the most important factors when selecting a light. Consider:

  • Room dimensions
  • Ceiling height
  • Furniture size
  • Table dimensions
  • Fixture diameter
  • Installation height
  • Visual balance

A fixture that looks perfect in a showroom may appear very different in a small room. Measure the space before ordering.

Pendant Lights vs. Ceiling Lights

Pendant lights hang from the ceiling and can provide both illumination and a strong decorative element. Ceiling-mounted fixtures sit closer to the ceiling and can be practical where ceiling height or available space is limited.

The choice depends on room dimensions, design preferences and installation requirements.

How Lighting Becomes a Design Feature

Lighting doesn't have to disappear into the background. A distinctive fixture can become a focal point in:

  • Dining rooms
  • Living rooms
  • Entryways
  • Bedrooms
  • Kitchens
  • Home offices

Consider the fixture's shape, material, scale and relationship to surrounding furniture.

Designer Lighting Buying Checklist

Before purchasing a light fixture, check:

  • Room dimensions

  • Fixture dimensions
  • Bulb requirements
  • Electrical compatibility
  • Installation requirements
  • Material
  • Cleaning requirements
  • Return policy
  • Overall design

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I choose the right size light fixture?
Consider the room dimensions, ceiling height, furniture and fixture proportions. Measure before ordering and compare the fixture dimensions with your space.
What type of lighting is best for a living room?
A combination of ambient, task and accent lighting can provide flexibility in a living room.
What lighting works over a dining table?
Pendant lights and chandeliers are common options. The appropriate size depends on the dining table and room dimensions.
Are pendant lights suitable for kitchens?
They can be useful over islands, counters or other areas, provided the fixture and installation are appropriate for the space.
How high should a pendant light hang?
The appropriate height depends on the room, ceiling height, fixture and what is underneath it. Follow the manufacturer's installation guidance.
What is the difference between ambient and accent lighting?
Ambient lighting provides general illumination, while accent lighting is typically used to highlight specific areas or objects.
How do I choose lighting for a small room?
Pay close attention to scale and available space. Fixtures that are too large can visually overwhelm smaller rooms.
What should I consider before buying designer lighting?
Check dimensions, electrical requirements, installation needs, bulb specifications, materials, maintenance and return policies.
