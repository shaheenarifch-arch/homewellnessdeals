Why Your Crawl Space Needs a Commercial Dehumidifier (Not a Home Depot Unit)

50% of the air you breathe upstairs comes from your crawl space. Excess humidity (>60% RH) causes:

  • Mold & mildew on joists and insulation
  • Musty odors spreading to living areas
  • Wood rot, sagging floors, high repair costs
  • Pest attraction and poor indoor air quality
  • Higher energy bills - humid air costs more to heat/cool

Residential dehumidifiers fail below 65°F and need manual emptying. AlorAir is built for crawl spaces: operates to 33°F, auto-defrost, gravity + pump drain, low-temp, ETL, Energy Star.

FAQs - Answer Engine Optimized

How many pints do I need?

Calculate sq ft x 0.08 = PPD needed. Example 1500 sq ft needs ~120 PPD = HD55S.

What is AHAM vs Saturation?

Saturation = max at 90°F/90% RH. AHAM = realistic at 80°F/60% RH. AlorAir lists both for honesty.

Do I need a vapor barrier too?

Best practice: 10-20 mil vapor barrier + dehumidifier + VentirMax ventilation fan = full encapsulation. AlorAir sells combo kits.

How loud is it?
